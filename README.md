# Klima Bosne i Hercegovine 1901–2099

Atlas Köppen-Geigerovih klimatskih tipova, temperature i padavina za Bosnu i Hercegovinu,
za historijske periode 1901–2020 i projekcije do 2099. po sedam SSP scenarija.

Analiza: Nedim Sladić, prateći Beck et al. (2018, 2023).

## Izvori
- Beck, H. E. i sar. (2023). High-resolution (1 km) Köppen-Geiger maps for 1901–2099 based on constrained CMIP6 projections. *Scientific Data* 10, 724.
- Beck, H. E. i sar. (2018). Present and future Köppen-Geiger climate classification maps at 1-km resolution. *Scientific Data* 5, 180214.
- E-OBS v31.0e (Cornes i sar., 2018), Copernicus Climate Change Service i ECA&D – dnevne temperature i padavine, za nekomercijalnu upotrebu.
- geoBoundaries (granica BiH), Esri World Shaded Relief (podloga karata).
